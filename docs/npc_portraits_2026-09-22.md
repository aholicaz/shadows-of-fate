# NPC dialogue portraits

Added portraits to 52 previously unassigned NPC instances across 13 maps. Existing custom portraits are preserved; Hans in Nidavellir reuses his original portrait. Other portraits are AtlasTexture regions of existing NPC artwork or the first idle frame, with no raster changes or duplicated images. Story variants keep their own art.

Dialogue portraits are capped at 32% viewport width to preserve text space.

Validation: checked all 52 references and NPC.line() textures, rendered Helga, Thjazi and Light Well dialogue previews; export boundary check passed. No real saves written.
